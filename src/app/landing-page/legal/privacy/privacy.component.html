Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Oxalate Info (oxalate.info) lets you look up oxalate levels in foods and, if you sign in, keep a daily food log. You can search foods without an account.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, and with Google sign-in also your name and profile photo. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Your food log and saved foods, so you can see them again when you sign in.
  • A profile photo you upload, if you add one. Anyone with the photo's link can view it.
  • Feedback you send: your name, email and message, which are emailed to us.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase records page visits and device information, using cookies. It is not linked to your account.
  • In your browser only: your theme, last search and a copy of your profile, kept in local storage.

PDF and CSV reports are created in your browser. Your totals are shared only if you choose a share option.

Google sign-in

We ask Google only for your name, email address and profile photo, and use them only for your account. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who we share it with

No one, except the services that run the site: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, storage, hosting and analytics) and EmailJS (feedback emails). We do not sell your data or show ads. Donation links go to PayPal, Venmo and Cash App, which handle payments under their own policies.

Deleting your data

You can remove foods from your log and saved list at any time. To delete your account and all of its data, ask us through the feedback page using your account's email address.

Other

The site is not meant for children under 13. If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions? Use the feedback page. See also our Terms of Use.
